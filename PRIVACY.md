# vClips Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 11, 2026_

vClips is a clipboard history manager for macOS. Your privacy is simple to
protect here, because **vClips collects nothing.**

## What vClips stores

- Text you copy is saved **only on your Mac**, in a local database
  (SwiftData). It never leaves your device.
- Content marked confidential by other apps (e.g. password managers using
  `org.nspasteboard.ConcealedType`) or transient is **never captured**.

## What vClips does NOT do

- No network access: vClips sends and receives no data.
- No analytics, telemetry, tracking, or crash reporting.
- No accounts, no identifiers, no third-party services.

## Permissions

- **Accessibility** (optional): used solely to synthesize the ⌘V keystroke
  that pastes your selected clip into the frontmost app. Without it, vClips
  falls back to copy-only.

## Data deletion

Delete items from the popup (⌘⌫), or remove all data by deleting the app's
local database. Uninstalling vClips removes everything.
